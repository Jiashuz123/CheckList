How points work
Every todo is worth 10 base points. Finish by your ideal date → +25 bonus (35 total). Between ideal and personal date the bonus shrinks each day. On the hard deadline day → −15 (−5 total). After → −20 (−10 total). Every point = $0.10 Pure Fun.
Example: homework due Monday, ideal Friday, personal Sunday → Fri 35 · Sat 28 · Sun 20 · Mon −5 · later −10.
